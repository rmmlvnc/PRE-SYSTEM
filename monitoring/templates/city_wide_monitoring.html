Data Interpretation
DengueWatch uses the same case-volume display thresholds used by the hotspot map: Low (1-5), Moderate (6-50), High (51-100), and Critical (101+). The page is intended to support monitoring, planning, alerts, and dengue prevention decisions. It is not a substitute for medical or epidemiological assessment.
