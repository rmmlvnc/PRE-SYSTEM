Risk Classification Guide

These are the same case-volume thresholds used by the DengueWatch hotspot map.


Low Risk

1 - 5 Cases

Dengue cases are present, but case volume is currently under the lowest DengueWatch risk classification.

LGU Focus: Routine monitoring, prevention reminders, community awareness, and environmental clean-up activities.
Moderate Risk

6 - 50 Cases

The barangay has an increased dengue case volume and should receive closer surveillance.

LGU Focus: Strengthen monitoring, clean-up drives, awareness campaigns, and coordination with health workers.
High Risk

51 - 100 Cases

The barangay has a high dengue case volume and may require prioritized public-health attention.

LGU Focus: Coordinate prevention measures with CHO, prioritize resources, strengthen alerts, and intensify monitoring.
Critical Risk

101+ Cases

The barangay is under the highest DengueWatch case-volume classification.

LGU Focus: Prioritize coordination with CHO, response planning, prevention activities, resource support, alerts, and assessment.
