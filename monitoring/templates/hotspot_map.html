Map Information

This map uses the dengue records contained in the loaded CSV datasets. Each record is grouped according to its reported Barangay and Sitio / Purok / Street Name.

The map uses official barangay boundaries for geographic visualization. Individual Sitio / Purok / Street locations are displayed in the barangay popup.

Risk levels are based on case-volume thresholds used by this dashboard. They are not an official DOH epidemiological classification.
