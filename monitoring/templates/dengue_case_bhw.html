Case Status Guide
Pending means the report is waiting for review.
Confirmed / Active means the case is currently counted as a dengue case in the assigned monitoring area.
Recovered remains part of the dengue case history but is no longer counted as a current dengue case.
Rejected means the submitted report was not accepted as a dengue case.
