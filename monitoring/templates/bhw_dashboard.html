DengueWatch Risk Levels

The dashboard uses the same display thresholds as the hotspot map. These are monitoring categories, not official DOH outbreak declarations.


Low
1 - 5 cases

Continue routine monitoring, prevention reminders, and environmental clean-up.

Moderate
6 - 50 cases

Increase monitoring and promptly report relevant dengue cases to the CHO.

High
51 - 100 cases

Give priority to monitoring, prevention activities, alerts, and coordination.

Critical
101+ cases

Immediately strengthen coordination with the City Health Office and prioritize prevention and response activities.

Decision-Support Notice
DengueWatch risk and hotspot information is intended to support surveillance and dengue prevention. It should not replace medical diagnosis or professional epidemiological assessment.
